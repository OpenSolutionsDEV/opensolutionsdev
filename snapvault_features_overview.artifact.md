# SnapVault — The Ultimate 100% Local AI Screenshot Vault & Smart Organizer

**SnapVault** is a privacy-first, local-first Android app that transforms your messy screenshot gallery into an organized, searchable, and secure personal vault. Powered by on-device intelligence, SnapVault automatically categorizes screenshots, extracts readable text, tracks receipt expenses, redacts sensitive info, and secures private collections—with **zero data ever leaving your phone**.

---

## Key Highlights & Core Value

* **100% On-Device Intelligence**: All text recognition (OCR), image classification, and search processing happen entirely on your phone. No cloud uploads, no server tracking, no account required.
* **Instant Search Across All Screenshot Text**: Search any word, number, amount, date, or hashtag buried inside your screenshots.
* **Biometric & AES-256 Encrypted Private Vault**: Hide and encrypt sensitive collections behind Fingerprint, Face Unlock, or Device PIN.
* **Automatic Financial Expense Tracking**: Automatically detects payment receipts, aggregates total spend, and exports PDF/CSV expense reports.
* **Built-in Image Editing Suite**: Crop out status bars, 1-tap redact sensitive info, and stitch multiple screenshots into a single seamless image.

## Detailed Feature Breakdown

### 1. Smart AI Categorization
SnapVault continuously organizes incoming screenshots into intelligent, dedicated categories:

* 💳 **Payments & Receipts**: Detects UPI transactions, bank transfers, Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm receipts with instant expense total calculation.
* 🛍️ **Shopping & Orders**: Captures order confirmations, delivery dates, e-commerce invoices from Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Swiggy, and Blinkit.
* ✈️ **Travel & Tickets**: Identifies boarding passes, IRCTC train tickets, flight bookings, PNR numbers, seat details, and gate numbers.
* 🙏 **Devotional & Sacred Content**: Recognizes mantras, prayers, scriptures, temple/shrine photos, and devotional quotes (including Hindu, Jain, Christian, Islamic, and Buddhist content).
* 📄 **Docs & Identification**: Groups Aadhaar cards, PAN cards, passports, resumes, certificates, and official documents.
* 💬 **Chats & Social Threads**: Organizes messaging screenshots, social media posts, and chat conversations.
* 💻 **Code & Technical Notes**: Groups code snippets, terminal commands, URLs, error logs, and tech documentation.
* 🧑 **People & Photos**: Separates selfies, portraits, and human photos.
* 😂 **Memes & Quotes**: Isolates viral memes, posters, and inspirational quotes.
* ⏳ **OTPs & Verification Codes**: Temporarily stores one-time passwords and security codes.
* 📁 **Custom Collections**: Create your own custom folders (e.g., *"Tax Receipts 2026"*, *"Goa Trip"*, *"Home Renovation"*) and group relevant screenshots together.

---

### 2. High-Accuracy Search Engine
Finding screenshots is as fast and easy as searching on the web:

* **Smart Number & Amount Normalization**:
  * Search `2000` to instantly match formatted amounts like `₹ 2,000.00` or `rs.2000`.
  * Search partial phone numbers, card digits, order IDs (`ORD-12345`), or hashtags (`#GoaTrip`).
* **Instant As-You-Type Results**: Matches keywords inside screenshot text, filenames, or custom collection names instantly.
* **30-Day Free Search & Unlimited Pro Search**: Search recent screenshots for free, or unlock Pro for unlimited all-time search history.

---

### 3. Collection-Level Biometric Lock & AES-256 Encrypted Storage
Keep your personal photos, financial documents, and private screenshots completely secure:

* **AES-256 Encryption**: When a collection is locked, its screenshots are encrypted into secure private internal storage and hidden completely from your phone's public Gallery and main Vault grid.
* **Biometric & PIN Protection**: Require Fingerprint, Face Unlock, or Device PIN to view, unlock, or delete locked collections.
* **Screen Protection**: Prevents screen capture and recording while viewing locked collections.
* **Auto-Relock Security**: Automatically locks encrypted collections as soon as the app is minimized or the screen turns off.
* **Safe Deletion & Restore**: Choose between restoring encrypted files back to your Gallery or permanently deleting them with 1 tap.
