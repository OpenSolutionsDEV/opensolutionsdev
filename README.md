## 📱 About SnapVault

**SnapVault** is a privacy-first, local-first Android application that transforms messy screenshot galleries into organized, searchable, and encrypted personal vaults. Powered by 100% on-device artificial intelligence:
- **Zero Cloud Uploads**: All on-device AI categorizations and text recognition happen on-device. Zero data leaves your phone.
- **Biometric & AES-256 Encrypted Vault**: Protect sensitive documents behind Fingerprint, Face Unlock, or PIN.
- **Instant Search**: Search any amount, number, hashtag, or text across all screenshots.
- **Expense Tracking**: Instant UPI and receipt detection with PDF/CSV expense report generation.
- **Built-in Image Suite**: Smart cropping, 1-tap text redaction, and screenshot stitching (vertical & horizontal).
